What it will not do

No diagnosis.
No treatment advice.
No invented certainty.

You control the record

Timeline events come from entries you and permitted family members create.

Files stay intact

Uploaded documents remain private files and are not automatically interpreted.

Clear medical boundary

LifeLens organizes information without medical conclusions.
